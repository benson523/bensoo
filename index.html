          ABOUT DELIVERANCE CHURCH MERU    

    BACKGROUND

    Deliverance Church Kenya was started in 1969 by the name Young Christian Ambassadors, Fellowship, derived from 2 Corinthians 5:20. The need to operate as a church was realised on 22nd November 1970. The name DELIVERANCE CHURCH derived from Luke 4:18-19 was preferred adopted immediately. This ministry was a spontaneous work of the Holy Spirit. The church has grown beyond the nation of kenya to the glory of God. The church in meru was born on sunday 26th July 1997 with an initial mandate to build an Apostolic Family Church with five primary objectives;
     

    1. To win souls to Christ

    2.To teach believers God's word

    3. To train effective church leaders

    4.To plant new testament Churches

    5.To establish Christian families

    6. Kingdom stewardship.





    OUR VISSION

    To be an Apostolic family Church for Kingdom services in Meru, kenya, Africa and the whole World. Acts 1:8

    OUR MISSION

    To do holistic community transformation through prayer, evangelism and discipleship.

    OUR CORE VALUES

    a. Unconditional love

    b. Stubborn faith

    c.Integrity of character

    d. Excelence in service

    e. Mutual accountability

    f. Innovative leadership

    g. Genuine fellowship

    OUR LEGACY GOAL

    To raise sons and daughters for kingdom service with a global mindset.

    OUR FAMILY SIGNATURE

    Prosperity for Generosity. We are blessed to be a blessing.
